# Head of Agentic Engineering, EverBetter LLC

EverBetter builds healthcare products on both sides of the HIPAA boundary: PRO and MED serve clinics under HIPAA; HEALTH is a direct-to-member sovereign app on Solid pods, where HIPAA does not apply. We position ourselves as the first sovereign AI company in healthcare.

You build our in-house orchestrator and make it the way every engineer ships, for both operational workflows and non-linear software development. You have hand-managed 10 or more concurrent AI agents and built the orchestrator that ran them. When any step slows delivery, you drop what you are doing and join it until it moves.

What you will own:

- The agent fleet: 10 or more concurrent agents you scope, review at the gates, and stop when KPIs miss.
- The orchestrator on NATS and JetStream: dispatch, state and checkpoints, idempotent retries, bounded fan-out, context management, tool and model routing, per-agent cost metering, tracing, a kill switch, and human review queues.
- Distribution: templates, starter agents, one-command setup, and a written playbook. Every practice a rule can express becomes a CI check. New engineers ship an agent-built change in week one.
- Dual-regime governance: one operating standard with a HIPAA profile and a sovereign profile, with the boundary enforced in code.
- Verification before build: functional (requirements traced to tests, contract tests, FHIR conformance) and non-functional (performance, reliability, security, maintainability, observability, token budget).
- SDLC gates: design validators, code review, regression on every model change, SecOps and DevOps release gates, and a checksummed evidence store in production.

KPIs you will report:

- TVR: lead time, deployment frequency, feature throughput per engineer, orchestrator adoption, and revenue enabled.
- TCO: token spend per feature, NRE burn against stage exits, reviewer minutes per change, change failure rate, and escaped defects.

What you bring:

- 10 or more concurrent agents hand-managed in production, with logs and cost ledgers.
- An orchestrator you designed and built yourself.
- Internal tooling you rolled out to a whole team, with adoption you measured.
- A bottleneck you cleared by leaving your own roadmap, with before and after numbers.
- Delivery under regulated and data-sovereign regimes.
- Equal depth in technology and business, and written, async-first communication.

How we evaluate:

1. Walk us through your orchestrator: one failure it caught and one it missed.
2. Show a week of hand-managing 10 or more agents: what you stopped, what you corrected, and why.
3. Scenario: the review queue is three days deep and a release is due. In writing, state what you drop, what you do first, and what you encode afterward.
