- Seating in **groups of four**, not rows
- [N] students = [N/4] tables

- Whiteboard or flip chart, if available
- Room access **20 minutes before** the start

**2. Display**

- Projector or large display, [HDMI / USB-C] input, presenting from a [MacBook Pro]

- [N] student laptops plus my machine, all online simultaneously

- Each student brings **their own laptop**
- Each student needs **one LLM account — free tier is sufficient**, any vendor
- No software installation, no administrator rights, no lab machines
- Nothing for the institution to license or purchase

*(I may bring a single shared API key for one part of the exercise. That is mine, not
something the institution provides, and the exercise works without it.)*

**Not required**

- Printing — all handouts are published online · lab computers · software installation · special network ports · recording · catering

**If a trade-off is forced:** prioritize display legibility, working student internet, and seating in fours over room capacity.

Reply by email is fine — no meeting needed.
