This is the second edition of this session, and the frame has moved. The first edition asked whether you could evaluate an agent project. This one asks something harder and more useful: what does an AI-first company actually want from a business person? Not somebody who is excited about AI. Somebody who can run a project whose underpinning is AI the way you would run any other capital-and-operating project — with a total cost of ownership, a bounded design budget, a forecast operating cost that includes both tokens and people, and a way of knowing next quarter whether any of it is still true. Six things along the bottom of this slide. By the end of the hour you will have produced the last one, on a real process of your own. (1 min)
