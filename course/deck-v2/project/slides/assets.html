Artifact four is the one that separates a project from a pilot. Five assets, and the point of the fourth column is that they are nearly free: they are files and scripts, they are deterministic, and not one of them calls a model. The cost ledger records every call with its meters and whether the price was measured or estimated. The unit register records every unit and which step decided it, which is how you get cost per completed decision instead of cost per request. The acceptance record says who signed for which design, chained so that a later edit is visible rather than silent — that is segregation of duties made mechanical. The evidence store holds the proof behind each proposal, checksummed, so a reviewer can check without trusting. And the pre-flight check is one command that asserts the whole system still behaves, which is what lets you make a release decision in a minute instead of a meeting. The second box is the part people get wrong: build these at stage two, while you are designing, not after go-live. Every one of them records something that cannot be reconstructed afterwards, and the first time you need them is the first time you have an incident. (3 min)
