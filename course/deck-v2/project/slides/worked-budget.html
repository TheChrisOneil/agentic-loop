A worked budget, from the system that runs this session. The top half is measured: thirty-four designs generated, twenty-nine of them priced by the tool that made the call, mean twenty-five and a half cents. Five are unpriced because they came back without usage data, and the ledger says so rather than guessing. The middle is the architecture doing its job: nine units enter, three are refused before any model call because a rule caught them, two close on arithmetic alone, and only four ever reach a judgment. That ratio is the whole of unit economics in one line. Then the two gold cells, and these matter more than the black ones. The price of those four calls is not metered here, because the judgment step in the demo is a deliberate stub — so I will not put a number on it, and neither should a vendor who has not measured yours.
