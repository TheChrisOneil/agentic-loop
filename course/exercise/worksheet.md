## The process

**Supplier invoices that don't match their purchase order.**

Your finance team receives roughly **400 supplier invoices a month**. A person opens each one,
finds the matching purchase order, and compares them line by line. Most match. The ones that
do not are a mixture of: a price that changed, a partial shipment, a line nobody ordered, a
duplicate that was already paid, and occasionally an invoice with no purchase order at all.

Nothing is paid without an approval. Approvals currently take nine days. Two people do this
full time, and one of them is leaving.

*(If your team would rather use its own process — refunds over $500, inbound résumés for one
role, contract renewals with changed terms — say so now and use that instead. The six boxes
are the same.)*
