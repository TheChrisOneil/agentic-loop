Steps 1 and 2 establish the TCO baseline. Steps 3 to 6 build artifact 6. Step 7 sets up artifact 4. Today you execute steps 3 through 6 on a live enterprise process.

This seven-step method is your practical framework for transforming business processes into scalable agentic systems. Step 3 is your highest-leverage decision: defining the precise unit of work. Step 5 isolates judgment so you pay for AI tokens only where strategic reasoning creates dynamic value.
