Expected OpEx — tokens and oversight as one equation

units per period × [ token cost + (review minutes × loaded rate) ] + fixed platform + continuous assurance

Tokens are the microscopic, dynamic half. Four meters: input, output, active reasoning, cached prompt memory. Precise metering makes the line predictable.

High-value talent is the steering wheel. Reviewers and approvers direct throughput rather than perform the work, magnifying individual impact.

Volume is the expansion driver. As cost per unit falls, transaction volume rises. Plan for the throughput growth, not just the rate.

Throughput is bounded by review capacity. Balance generation against oversight, or the system builds a queue instead of a benefit.

Operating cost unites low-cost digital compute with high-yield human expertise. Token costs are tiny and continuously falling; human oversight is the strategic steering that protects quality. When an agentic harness makes executing a task ninety percent cheaper, demand for that task explodes, which is a top-line opportunity and a capacity problem at the same time. Manage capacity so your sharpest managers are freed from repetitive execution. (2 min)
