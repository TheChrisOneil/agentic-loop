Every person here has used a chatbot with their prompt portfolio. Today is not about that. Today is about the single biggest disruption to the enterprise: the Agentic Harness. That is an entirely different discipline, and it is the next frontier of durable corporate intellectual property. Enterprise valuation isn't built on rented prompts or generic AI models. The real corporate IP is the harness — the proprietary architecture, skills and processes that turn organizational intelligence into repeatable, defensible and hyper-profitable business execution. This harness bridges two worlds: high-level cognitive pipelines that reason through non-linear problems like software creation, and operational execution that runs core workflows with deterministic, RPA-like reliability. AI is a permanent accelerant — our leap from stage coach to commercial flight. Your mandate is leveraging the harness to achieve 10x growth, velocity and margin expansion. Winning requires managing the entire lifecycle: maximizing Total Value Realized, controlling Total Cost of Ownership, and empowering your talent to direct these systems. As a CTO, I build from the ground up. So while I acknowledge the limitless ceiling of cognitive pipelines, today we zoom in on operational execution and TCO. By the end of this session, you will draw an autonomous harness on paper and defend where your IP resides.
