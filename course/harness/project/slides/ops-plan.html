The five stage gates did not disappear. They became commands.

Human judgment stays where it matters — requirements and final acceptance. Machines handle structural verification, on every iteration rather than once a quarter.

We aren't eliminating operational rigor, we are automating it. Traditionally, moving through five design review gates took months of calendar coordination and paper sign-offs. In an agentic framework, gates three and four run in seconds via machine checks, on every iteration, leaving immutable logs. Leaders stay firmly in control where judgment matters: setting strategic intent at the start and giving final approval at release. (3 min)
