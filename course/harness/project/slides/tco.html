Two layers are one-time capital. Five recur. A business case that accounts only for licenses and tokens is a quote.

To capture true 10x financial spread, you must evaluate the complete TCO stack across all seven layers. Token charges are typically the smallest line item on this ledger; high-value human oversight and platform assurance represent the true operational core. Take layer five: when vendors release faster, smarter frontier models, re-verifying your workflow isn't an unexpected emergency — it's a scheduled performance upgrade that unlocks even higher throughput. By framing the full TCO from womb to tomb, you ensure your business case delivers sustainable, hyper-profitable yield year after year. (4 min)
