The engineering J-curve — protect the capacity that builds the harness

Harness engineering is unfamiliar work with almost no market experience behind it. Told only to do more with less, teams default to ad-hoc scripts, which is technical debt rather than IP.

Stop saying “do more with less” without giving structural leverage. Buy the time to build the harness, and the returns compound.

Let's be fair and realistic about engineering teams, because as a CTO I see this trap everywhere. Most leaders throw AI at their tech teams and say do more with less, build it faster. Harness engineering is counter-intuitive, deeply complex, and there is almost zero market experience for it. Demand instant feature output without giving engineers space to understand harness tooling and they default to what they know: quick ad-hoc scripts and hacked prompts. That creates technical debt, not corporate IP. You have to lobby your executive peers and the board for dedicated NRE aircover, and you sell them on the J-curve. Yes, velocity dips while engineers master harness architecture and build reusable skill packs. That is an intentional capital investment. Once the tooling is in place the exchange rate explodes: work that took many engineering hours of custom coding becomes a validated operational design in about thirty seconds for twenty-six cents. Don't just mandate harness tooling — buy your engineers the time to build it right. (3 min)
