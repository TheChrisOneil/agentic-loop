SOX §404 · 21 CFR Part 11 · HIPAA §164.312. And the new one: a vendor’s model update is a logic change. Log the model version beside the design, and re-run verification on every update.

For leaders in public companies or regulated industries, compliance is non-negotiable. The harness embeds regulatory verification directly into execution: every modification, approval and output leaves an immutable, tamper-evident trail. The genuinely new problem is the last line. When an external vendor upgrades a model, that is a change to your control environment that nobody sends you a letter about. Treat it as a change event and re-verify automatically. Very few companies do this yet, and it is going to be an audit finding. (3 min)
