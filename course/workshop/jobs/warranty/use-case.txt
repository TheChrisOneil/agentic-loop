We handle warranty claims on returned hardware. About 250 claims a month arrive through the
dealer portal. Each claim has a serial number, a fault description written by the dealer, a
purchase date, and sometimes photographs.

Two technicians work the queue. For each claim they check whether the serial is still inside
its warranty window, whether the fault described is a covered fault or wear and tear, and
whether the same serial has been claimed before. Most claims are straightforward: the dates
line up and the fault is on the covered list.

The hard ones are where the dealer's description is ambiguous, or where the photographs
suggest impact damage that the description does not mention. Those need somebody who knows the
product to look. Roughly one in six.

Claims over 2000 dollars go to the regional manager regardless. We have had two cases where a
dealer submitted the same serial twice under different claim numbers and both were paid.

Today it takes about eight days from submission to a decision, and the technicians say the
queue is the worst part of their job.
