We reconcile supplier invoices against purchase orders. Roughly 400 invoices a month arrive
from about forty suppliers, and a person opens each one, finds the matching purchase order,
and compares them line by line.

Most match exactly. The ones that do not are a mixture: a price that changed since the order
was raised, a partial shipment, a line nobody ordered, a duplicate that was already paid, and
occasionally an invoice with no purchase order at all.

Two people do this full time. They tell us the worst part is that everything looks the same
until you have opened it, so the routine ones cost as much attention as the exceptions.

Small variances are not worth anyone's time — under fifty dollars and under two percent, we
just pay them. Anything above two thousand dollars needs a second approver whatever it is.
Some suppliers are on a list where a clean reconciliation is still not enough authority to pay,
because of how the contract is written.

We have twice paid the same invoice twice when a supplier resubmitted it under a new number.
Nothing is paid without an approval, and approvals currently take nine days.
