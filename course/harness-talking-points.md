## 1. The Agentic Harness

`cover`

Every person here has used a chatbot with their prompt portfolio. Today is not about that. Today is about the single biggest disruption to the enterprise: the Agentic Harness. The real corporate IP is the harness — the proprietary architecture, skills and processes that turn organizational intelligence into repeatable, defensible and hyper-profitable business execution. This harness bridges two worlds: high-level cognitive pipelines that reason through non-linear problems like software creation, and operational execution that runs core workflows with deterministic, RPA-like reliability. AI is a permanent accelerant — our leap from stage coach to commercial flight. Your mandate is leveraging the harness to achieve 10x growth, velocity and margin expansion. Winning requires managing the entire lifecycle: maximizing Total Value Realized, controlling Total Cost of Ownership, and empowering your talent to direct these systems. As a CTO, I build from the ground up. So while I acknowledge the limitless ceiling of cognitive pipelines, today we zoom in on operational execution and TCO. ## 2. We are not looking for AI enthusiasts. We are empowering leaders who direct enterprise scale and build agentic IP.

We don't need AI evangelists; we need business architects who balance predictable TCO against exponential TVR while building proprietary Agentic Harness IP for the enterprise. (3 min)

## 5. Deterministic by default. Probabilistic where it creates exponential value.

`determinism`

This is the single most critical slide in the deck for maximizing your business yield. It isn't a technical distinction — it's a capital allocation strategy. Deterministic steps cost zero per run once coded, deliver absolute reliability, and satisfy auditors instantly. Probabilistic steps give you transformative cognitive reasoning, but charge you per execution. The key to winning is simple: default to deterministic code for routine logic, and deploy AI intelligence precisely where it acts as a massive accelerant. If two competent managers always handle a step the exact same way, that is a business rule, not an AI prompt. Converting unwritten rules into code drops your operating floor permanently and allows you to reinvest those tokens into exponential, top-line TVR opportunities. (4 min)

Stage 1 sets an indisputable baseline so every future efficiency win is mathematically measurable. Stage 2 is the deep architectural work that unlocks the leverage. ## 12. The engineering J-curve — protect the capacity that builds the harness

`engineer-roi`

Let's be fair and realistic about engineering teams, because as a CTO I see this trap everywhere. Most leaders throw AI at their tech teams and say do more with less, build it faster. Harness engineering is counter-intuitive, deeply complex, and there is almost zero market experience for it. Demand instant feature output without giving engineers space to understand harness tooling and they default to what they know: quick ad-hoc scripts and hacked prompts. That creates technical debt, not corporate IP. You have to lobby your executive peers and the board for dedicated NRE aircover, and you sell them on the J-curve. Yes, velocity dips while engineers master harness architecture and build reusable skill packs. That is an intentional capital investment. Once the tooling is in place the exchange rate explodes: work that took many engineering hours of custom coding becomes a validated operational design in about thirty seconds for twenty-six cents. Don't just mandate harness tooling — buy your engineers the time to build it right. (3 min)

## 16. Automated design controls turn audit into an advantage

`traceability`

For leaders in public companies or regulated industries, compliance is non-negotiable. When an external vendor upgrades a model, that is a change to your control environment that nobody sends you a letter about. Treat it as a change event and re-verify automatically. ## 17. The executive scorecard

`kpis`

Every metric on this scorecard originates in an automated governance asset; a KPI with no asset behind it is an intention. Cost per completed decision measures financial leverage. ## 18. Managing the system continuously to hold the yield

`cadence`

Continuous governance maintains the advantage long after go-live. Monthly tracking of unit financial yield, quarterly review of oversight gains, and instant automated re-verification whenever a vendor updates a model. Pre-assign accountable, single-threaded leaders by name, and set the exit threshold before launch. Writing that number down while everyone is still optimistic is the clearest signal a proposal is honest. (2 min)
